Pitch 1 · replaces three scripts and three bar icons

Logbook

One On Air lamp that is always truthful about your mic, and every meeting turns into a transcript without you opening a terminal.

Today, confirmed on this machine

Stopping the meeting recorder does not stop it. stop_recording sends kill -- -$loop_pid to a process group that doesn't exist, then pkill -P kills only the loop's children. The loop wakes up and starts the next pw-record chunk while the bar icon has already gone dark. A copy of the script with sleep standing in for the mic kept recording five seconds after "stop".

The pid files also live in /tmp, which on this desktop is on disk and survives a reboot. A crash mid-recording leaves the red icon lit, and the next click tries to stop a recording that isn't there. Transcribing is a manual meeting-record.sh transcribe <dir> run in a terminal.

Try a state:
mock · recordings listed are examples

What it does

  • One lamp replaces the dictation, meeting and screen icons: dim when the mic is closed, red with a running clock while anything records, amber while transcripts are being made.
  • It watches PipeWire, so the lamp also lights when Zoom, Chrome or anything else has your mic open, and the drawer says who.
  • Stop really stops. The daemon owns the recorder processes and kills them on stop or on its own exit, and its state lives in $XDG_RUNTIME_DIR, which a reboot clears.
  • On stop, transcription queues itself in Switchyard as a heavy job, so it waits for a running build and runs under lowprio.
  • A macro key drops a "mark" into the meeting. Marks show up as headings in the transcript so you can find the decision that was made at minute 31.
  • Meetings turn on do not disturb (which also hides the song title), the same way screen recording already does.

Time back

At your numbers: 20 min a week.

Every recorded meeting today ends with: open a terminal, find the dated folder under ~/Audio/meetings, run the transcribe, watch it, open the file. In Logbook the transcript is waiting in the drawer with a Copy button by the time you've refilled your coffee.

The bigger saving is the one you can't put a number on: never wondering if the mic is still live after a call.

Work

Meetings become text you can search, paste into notes or hand to an agent for a summary and action items.

Privacy

The lamp tells the truth about every app that has the mic, not only your own recorder.

Game dev

Screen captures of a Detour 33 or Everspark build land in one list with their length, ready to drag into a devlog.

The machine

Transcribing a long meeting is heavy. Queued behind builds, it never fights a Gradle run for RAM.

Why you'd pay for itYou stop worrying that the mic is still recording, and you never open a terminal to get a transcript again.

Pitch 2 · replaces the clock and the pomodoro

Timetable

The clock tells you what's next, puts the join button one click away, and turns the free gaps in your day into focus blocks.

Today

The clock shows one zone at a time. Seeing EET means clicking, which runs pgrep -f to find the script and sends it a signal, then clicking twice more to get back. Right-click opens gnome-calendar, which doesn't show your work calendar's join links. The pomodoro runs its 25 minutes whether or not a meeting starts in 10.

Try a state:
mock · events are examples, times are real offsets for Nov 10

What it does

  • The bar reads "Standup in 7m": calm when the next thing is an hour off, amber at 10 minutes, pulsing red at start time.
  • Join switches to workspace 2 (Work Slack + Zoom) and opens the Zoom or Meet link it found in the event.
  • Chicago, EET and GMT sit side by side in the drawer. No cycling.
  • Free gaps on the day strip are focus blocks. Start one and the timer sizes itself to the gap, then warns you 2 minutes before the next meeting. This absorbs the pomodoro.
  • Notifications at 5 and 1 minutes out go through swaync, so do not disturb still silences them.

Time back

At your numbers: 20 min a week.

Most of it is the small hunt before each meeting: open the calendar tab, find the event, find the link, switch workspace, join a minute late. Timetable makes that one click from where your eyes already are.

The focus blocks matter more than the minutes. A timer that knows your calendar stops you starting deep work you'll have to drop in 10 minutes.

Work

On time to every call without keeping a calendar tab open on workspace 1.

Focus

Pomodoros that fit the real gaps in your day.

People abroad

The EET and GMT times are on screen when you're picking a slot for someone overseas.

Evenings

After your last event the bar says so, which is a nicer cue to stop than the clock.

Why you'd pay for itYou're never the one who joins two minutes late from the wrong workspace, and focus time stops colliding with meetings.

Pitch 3 · the bar itself, config and CSS only

Platform

The same bar, laid out so it reads in one glance and makes the screenshot.

Today

Sixteen modules, each with a 1px divider, flush against the screen edge so the 6px corner radius never shows. "Off" icons (key lights, VPN, notifications) use the same green as healthy things, so a glance can't tell on from off. Two colors are from Dracula, not Nord: #50fa7b and #b8e0ea. 18px text in a 24px bar is cramped.

Compare:
after · click the arrow to open the toggles
  1. 1Float it. An 8px margin matching gaps_out, so the bar sits on the wallpaper like the windows do.
  2. 2Pills, not dividers. Workspaces, window, then media, lamps, audio, toggles, clock, tray. Four groups to read, not sixteen.
  3. 3Toggles in a drawer. Key lights, VPN, notifications and power collapse behind one arrow (waybar group with drawer). Anything that is on stays visible.
  4. 4One palette rule. Off is dim, on is the accent, and red only ever means "needs you". Every color from Nord.
  5. 5Room to breathe. A 34px bar with 15px text, and workspace icons that carry a small count of windows.
  6. 6Glass, without the halo. Blur only on layer surfaces (the bar and the drawers), so Slack's menus stay clean.
Glance speed

Anything that isn't dim is something that's on. You read state from color alone.

Pitch 4 · replaces currently-playing.sh

Tannoy

Now playing with the cover art, a scrub bar and the right player, and still invisible on a screen share.

Today

Every field is cut at 20 characters mid-word, which is why the bar reads "StarTalk - Why We Haven't Reach". With Chrome and Spotify both open, playerctl picks one and you don't choose which. There's no position, no skip for podcasts, and five processes start every 3 seconds to draw it.

Try a state:
mock · sample track and placeholder art

What it does

  • A 16px cover beside a title that ends in a real ellipsis, with a thin progress line under it.
  • The drawer: big art, scrubbing, and ±15s skips that appear when the track is a podcast.
  • Player chips. Pick Spotify over the YouTube tab once and it stays picked.
  • The output device sits in the drawer too, using the same switch as the volume module.
  • Do not disturb still hides the title, and now the drawer says why it's hidden.
  • MPRIS over D-Bus with change signals: it redraws when the track changes, not every 3 seconds.

Time back

Honestly: seconds.

Picking the right player and scrubbing a podcast without alt-tabbing to Spotify saves a few seconds, several times a day. This one is about how the desk feels and looks, and the pitch should say so.

Listening

Podcasts get skips, music gets art, and the right app answers the media keys.

Screen shares

The privacy rule you already rely on keeps working, now with a visible reason.

Riding along

You called these maintenance, and they are. Each one is waybar config, not a new program, and fits in a single commit.

Stop polling

About 500 process launches a minute, by my count from the config, mostly printing nothing. swaync-client -swb streams do not disturb, playerctl --follow streams tracks until Tannoy exists, and the two record icons go away with Logbook.

App icons on workspaces

window-rewrite in hyprland/workspaces shows what's open where, and title rewrites turn nate@FROSTYARCH:~/Sites/dot-files into an icon and a folder name.

Layer blur

Blur for the bar and drawer namespaces only. Window blur stays off because of the Slack menu halo.

The hotkey sheet

Switchyard's shelf already lists your binds (binds = true). SUPER+/ still opens the old hotkeys.sh, and it and commands.sh can be deleted.

Suggested order: the two-line stop fix today, then Platform if the post comes first or Logbook if the time does. Timetable next, then Tannoy, which looks best on the new Platform anyway.
